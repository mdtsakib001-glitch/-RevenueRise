About PathSoft

Our Story

PathSoft started ten years ago as a three-person consultancy solving one client's infrastructure headache. That client referred another, and the work — corporate systems, call-center platforms, cloud migrations — kept compounding.

Today we're a full-service IT partner, but the instinct hasn't changed: understand the business problem first, then build the smallest reliable thing that solves it.
